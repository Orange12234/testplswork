Chess piece artwork
===================

The twelve piece images in this folder (wK.svg ... bP.svg) are the "cburnett" piece
set, drawn by Colin M.L. Burnett (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User:Cburnett).

They were taken from the npm package "chessground" 9.2.1
(https://www.npmjs.com/package/chessground), which is distributed under the
GNU General Public License, version 3 or later. The licence text is in COPYING.txt
next to this file. The only change made to the images is adding a viewBox attribute
to the <svg> tag so that they scale to any size.

These SVG files are the complete source of the artwork. This credit applies only to
the files in this folder, not to the rest of the site.
